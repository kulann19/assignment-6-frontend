Effect methods

Method guide

  • .hide() hides an element.
  • .show() displays an element.
  • .toggle() switches visibility.
  • .fadeIn() gradually shows an element.
  • .fadeOut() gradually hides it.
  • .slideUp() and .slideDown() animate height.
